patch10498.patch
  Extends the soffset-split safety check to peel sign-preserving
  transparent wrappers (splat/broadcast/expand_dims/reshape/convert_layout)
  before trusting the integer-range lattice, so an offset of the form
  convert_layout(add(base, <negative>)) is no longer wrongly annotated
  split-safe. Mirrors upstream Triton PR
  https://github.com/triton-lang/triton/pull/10498 exactly.

  Note: previously applied on top of patch10450; patch10450 (and patch10290)
  landed upstream as of the main-windows bump (Triton 924eda62f4), so both
  were dropped. patch10498 still applies cleanly on its own.
